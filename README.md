# Joseph W. Anady

Founder and Principal Engineer of [ThatDeveloperGuy](https://thatdeveloperguy.com/), the trade name of THATDEVELOPERGUY LLC. The company is service-disabled veteran-owned, works from Cassville, Missouri, and has been in business since 2017.

I build websites and the software behind them, run them on a server the company owns, and do the search and AI visibility work that helps them get found.

The public record of the company and of me, with the evidence behind each entry, is at [feedthejoe.com/joseph](https://feedthejoe.com/joseph/).
